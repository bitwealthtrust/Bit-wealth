Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Bit-Wealth, our services and our approach to financial information.

What is Bit-Wealth?
Bit-Wealth is a digital finance information platform focused on financial education, digital finance topics and accessible financial resources.
What services does Bit-Wealth provide?
Bit-Wealth provides educational information covering financial concepts, digital finance, investment knowledge, digital assets and financial technology.
Does Bit-Wealth guarantee investment returns?
No. Bit-Wealth does not guarantee profits, investment returns or financial outcomes. Financial markets and digital assets can involve significant risk.
Is Bit-Wealth financial advice?
Information published on Bit-Wealth is intended for general educational purposes and should not be treated as personalized financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
Does Bit-Wealth manage customer investments?
The current Bit-Wealth website is designed as an informational and educational platform. It does not claim to manage customer investments or hold customer funds.
What are digital assets?
Digital assets are assets represented in digital form. Examples can include cryptocurrencies and other blockchain-based assets. Their value can change significantly and they may involve substantial risks.
How can I contact Bit-Wealth?
You can use the Contact page to send a general enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information through the website.
Where can I learn more?
Visit our Services and About pages to learn more about Bit-Wealth and the financial topics covered by the platform.

Important Information

Financial products and digital assets can involve risk, including the potential loss of money. Always consider your circumstances and seek qualified professional advice where appropriate.
